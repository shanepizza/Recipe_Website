Mac and Cheese Recipe

Under construction

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces elbow macaroni

    • Cheese Sauce

  • 2 Tablespoons salted butter
  • 2 Tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • ½ teaspoon sea salt
  • ¼ teaspoon garlic powder (optional but recommended)
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • ¼ cup sour cream or Greek yogurt
  • 8 ounces shredded cheddar cheese (2 cups)

Instructions

  1. Cook the elbow macaroni according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a medium saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat. Stir in the flour and cook the roux for 1-2 minutes until lightly golden.
  3. Gradually whisk in the milk and cook until the sauce thickens, about 5-7 minutes.
  4. Remove from heat and stir in the sour cream and shredded cheddar cheese until smooth.
  5. Combine the cooked macaroni with the cheese sauce. Serve immediately.
